i About these violation counts

These are official HPD violations — reported, inspected, and confirmed by the city. But violation counts only reflect what's been reported and caught, not a building's actual pest conditions. A building with 2 violations could have a worse infestation than one with 8, if tenants haven't reported it or an inspector hasn't caught it yet. Use this list to identify strong leads, not to rank buildings by severity.
